Missed Call Widget (MCW) makes a missed call hard to overlook. The small missed-call symbol in the status bar is easy to miss, especially without your glasses. This widget shows the same thing in a size you choose yourself.

* Red, with a handset and an arrow bouncing away, when there are missed calls you have not looked at
* Grey and discreet when everything has been seen
* Tap the widget to open the phone app's list of recent calls; the widget turns grey again
* It also turns grey when you look at the calls directly in the phone app
* Orange when the permission to read the call log is missing
* Resizable: the handset lies down when the widget is wider than it is tall and stands up otherwise

The app needs permission to read the call log, to see whether there are missed calls, and the phone state, to notice when a call ends. Nothing leaves the phone: the app has no network access, no ads and no tracking.

The app's own screens are in Swedish. Requires Android 12 or later.
